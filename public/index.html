How a trip to Hawaii ended up on Spring Street

It started with one cup that blew our minds.

In 2017, Julie and Don Martin tried shave ice for the first time on a trip to Hawaii — ice cream in the bottom, soft fluffy ice, fruit syrups on top. They came home, bought an ice shaver, and started making it in their own kitchen.

Don grew up making ice cream with his family and was set on recreating that macadamia nut. Twenty-plus years behind a bar turned out to be the perfect training for building syrups from real fruit. And Maine? Maine didn't have anything like it.

So in 2020 they rolled out a cart. In 2021 they signed the lease on the West End shop. They've been shaving snow in Portland ever since — winter included.

— Julie & Don
